🧠 How The Hybrid Algorithm Works

Colab Notebook Architecture
📊

1. Implicit Engagement Score

Transforms raw watch time, completion ratio, likes, shares, and skip penalties into normalized 1–5 implicit ratings.

🧮

2. Truncated SVD

Decomposes sparse user-video interactions into 20 latent factors to capture hidden behavioral patterns.

🧬

3. Content Affinity Boost

Computes each user's historical category preferences and applies an explicit multiplier (+0.5 × mean rating).

🎯

4. Unseen Video Ranking

Filters out previously viewed content and ranks new candidate videos by Final Rec Score (CF + Affinity Boost).
